# Trigger Cadence - Worked Example

Pairs with the `trigger-cadence` skill. Fictional sender: **ACME Insight Studio**.

## Trigger list

| Account | Trigger (last 30 days) | POV sentence | Route |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fabrikam Health | Hiring 3 BI analysts | New analysts will spend their first month rebuilding reports by hand unless the reporting layer is already in place. | Medium · 4 touches / 14 days |
| Tailspin Travel | New VP of Operations | A new ops leader usually resets the weekly metrics review in their first 90 days. | Slow · 3 touches / 21 days |

## Cadence: Fabrikam Health (hiring trigger)

**Touch 1 · Email · Day 1**
> Subject: your three new BI analysts
>
> Hi Dana, saw Fabrikam is hiring three BI analysts. Teams we work with found new analysts spent weeks rebuilding recurring reports before doing new analysis. ACME Insight Studio cut that to two days at Northwind. Want the onboarding checklist they used? It's one page.

**Touch 2 · Email · Day 4** — the checklist, attached, with one line on which step saved the most time.

**Touch 3 · LinkedIn · Day 8** — short note referencing the job post and the checklist.

**Touch 4 · Email · Day 14** — a 15-minute offer to map their first-month reporting plan.

## Keep-warm and handoff rules

- No reply after touch 4, or the roles are filled → move to nurture for 60 days
- Reply at any point → hand to the account executive the same day

**Swap test:** each touch names Fabrikam's hiring or its consequence, so none of it would make sense sent to Tailspin.
