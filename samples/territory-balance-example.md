# Territory Balance - Worked Example

Pairs with the `territory-balance` skill. Fictional team: three ACME Insight Studio account executives, 60 mid-market accounts.

## Account potential (sample rows)

Potential = whitespace × propensity

| Account | Whitespace | Propensity | Potential | Tier |
|---|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| Northwind Logistics | $400K | 0.60 | $240K | A |
| Contoso Retail | $250K | 0.48 | $120K | A |
| Fabrikam Health | $150K | 0.40 | $60K | B |
| Tailspin Travel | $80K | 0.25 | $20K | C |

Touch norms: A = 8 / month, B = 3 / month, C = 1 / month. Capacity: 120 touches per rep per month.

## Balance table

Total potential $3.00M, mean $1.00M per rep. Band: ±10% of mean potential, load 0.75-0.90.

| Rep | Potential | vs mean | Touches / month | Load |
|---|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| Rep 1 | $1.02M | +2% | 98 | 0.82 |
| Rep 2 | $0.99M | -1% | 94 | 0.78 |
| Rep 3 | $0.99M | -1% | 103 | 0.86 |

## Exception log

| Account | Kept with | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Contoso Retail | Rep 2 | Mid-cycle opportunity; relationship owner since Q1 |

**Reading the result:** every rep sits within 2% of mean potential and inside the load band, with one logged exception (under 2% of accounts).
